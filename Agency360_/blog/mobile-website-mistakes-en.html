Mobile experience

Mobile Website Mistakes That Make People Leave

A website can look good on a large screen and still be frustrating on a phone. Start by testing real tasks—finding a service, reading a price, or sending an inquiry—at different screen sizes.

Informational article. Check current platform policies and rules that apply to your business.

Hard-to-read text and small controls

Check font size, line length, and contrast on light and dark backgrounds. Text over photos needs a sufficient background or overlay; color alone should not communicate a state.

Give buttons enough size and spacing to tap, and label them with the result they trigger. Avoid tiny, crowded links and form fields that extend beyond the screen.

Slow loading and confusing navigation

Large images are a common avoidable cause of delays. Resize and compress photos, use suitable formats, and lazy-load below-the-fold images when appropriate. Review fonts and third-party scripts that are not essential.

Keep navigation brief and predictable. Visitors should be able to go back, find contact details, and close menus easily; do not hide essential information behind full-screen pop-ups.

Test complete tasks, not just screenshots

On a real phone, open the site over a mobile connection and complete tasks: find a service, tap the phone number, fill in a form, and confirm it arrived. Repeat in more than one browser if possible.

Measure issues with privacy-conscious tools. Prioritize fixes affecting the most people and retest after updates, especially when changing a theme, widget, or form.

A practical checklist

  • Readable text and sufficient contrast
  • Easy-to-tap buttons
  • Optimized images
  • Usable menus and forms
  • Calls and submissions tested end to end

Frequently asked questions

How can I test my website on mobile?

Open it on multiple devices or use your browser's responsive tools, then complete real tasks instead of only looking at the layout.

What should I fix first?

Start with issues that block a task: unreadable text, untappable links, broken forms, or non-working contact buttons.
