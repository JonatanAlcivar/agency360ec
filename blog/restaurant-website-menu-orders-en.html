Restaurant Websites: Menus, Online Orders, and Reservations

When someone chooses where to eat, they need quick answers: what is on the menu, what it costs, where the restaurant is, and how to order. Your website can bring those details together and guide customers to the ordering channel you actually use.

Informational article. Check current platform policies and rules that apply to your business.

Publish a menu customers can read

Use selectable text instead of a blurry photo or a PDF that is difficult to read on a phone. Organize dishes by category and keep names, descriptions, and prices current. Mark dietary options or allergens only when your team has verified the information.

Assign someone to update availability, prices, and specials. If dishes change often, show when the menu was updated or use a platform staff can manage without a full rebuild.

Choose how orders and reservations work

Decide whether customers order by phone, WhatsApp, your own tool, or an external platform. Explain steps, delivery areas, hours, and payment methods before sending someone to another screen.

For reservations, show available times, party size, and how requests are confirmed or changed. Test the full flow and do not call a request a confirmed booking if it still needs approval.

Make essential details easy to find

Include the address, map, phone, hours, and useful parking or accessibility details. Keep this information consistent with public profiles and update it for holiday hours.

Optimize images for faster loading and make buttons easy to tap. Measure call or order clicks in a privacy-conscious way and compare which channels create orders your team can fulfill.

A practical checklist

  • Text menu with current prices
  • Verified allergen details
  • Clear order and reservation steps
  • Accurate hours and location
  • Ordering tested on a phone

Frequently asked questions

Should I use a third-party app or direct ordering?

It depends on fees, operations, and customer convenience. Make external steps clear and keep a backup contact method.

Can customers order through WhatsApp?

Yes, if your team can respond and manage orders there. Provide instructions, service hours, and a clear order confirmation.
