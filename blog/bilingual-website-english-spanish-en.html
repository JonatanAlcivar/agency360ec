Bilingual websites

Does Your Business Need a Website in English and Spanish?

A site in two languages can better serve customers who prefer English or Spanish, but only when each version is complete and maintainable. Base the decision on your audience and your ability to review content rather than assuming every business must translate every page.

Informational article. Check current platform policies and rules that apply to your business.

Decide based on your customers and services

Review the questions customers ask, the language they use, and the areas where you operate. If a meaningful share of your audience seeks information in both languages, a bilingual experience can reduce confusion about services, hours, pricing, and contact options.

You can also start with essential pages in both languages and expand later. A small, useful, reviewed version is better than many automatically translated pages that quickly become outdated.

Make the experience easy to navigate

Provide a visible language selector and links to equivalent pages. Keep business names, services, contact details, and terms consistent while adapting wording when a literal translation sounds unnatural.

Avoid mixing languages in the same navigation or changing content automatically without letting visitors choose. Label each page with its language and use separate URLs for language versions when your platform supports them.

Plan for review, SEO, and maintenance

Assign someone to review each language, especially prices, terms, forms, and legal messages. When a service changes, update both versions and test the links and form notifications.

Searches in each language use different phrases. Research how customers describe your services and write useful titles and copy for each audience. Language annotations help describe pages but cannot guarantee visibility or rankings on their own.

A practical checklist

  • Real demand in both languages
  • Visible language selector
  • Equivalent pages and clear links
  • Human review of content
  • A process to keep both versions current

Frequently asked questions

Is machine translation enough?

It can be a draft, but review it before publishing. Services, prices, and calls to action should sound natural and remain accurate.

Do I need to translate the whole site on day one?

Not always. Prioritize the home, services, contact, and pages receiving traffic; add content when you can maintain it.
