What Should a Contractor or Roofing Website Include?

Someone looking for a contractor usually wants to know whether you do the work they need, whether you serve their area, and how to ask a question. A clear website reduces steps and lets people evaluate your business before calling.

Informational article. Check current platform policies and rules that apply to your business.

Explain services and service areas

Organize roofing, repairs, remodeling, or plumbing into sections that are easy to browse. Describe which projects you accept and what information customers should prepare for a visit or estimate.

List the cities or neighborhoods where you genuinely work. Clarify boundaries, seasonal limits, or visit requirements. Do not present a residential address as a public office if customers cannot visit it.

Show projects and verifiable trust signals

Use original project photos with client permission and a short explanation of the issue, work completed, and general area. Do not publish names, addresses, or private details without authorization.

List licenses, insurance, certifications, and warranties only when current and verifiable. Explain each credential and link to an official source when helpful; avoid unsupported claims such as being the best or promising outcomes you cannot control.

Make estimate requests straightforward

Add clear options to call or request a quote. An initial form can ask for a name, contact method, type of work, approximate location, and a description without collecting unnecessary details.

Test forms on a phone, confirm inquiries reach a monitored inbox, and set honest response-time expectations. Add appropriate privacy notices or consent when you collect personal information.

A practical checklist

  • Services described in plain language
  • Accurate service area
  • Authorized original photos
  • Verifiable credentials
  • Form tested on mobile

Frequently asked questions

Should I publish prices?

When cost depends on an inspection or materials, explain how estimates work and what affects pricing. Show starting prices only when accurate and clearly qualified.

Do I need one page per city?

Only when you can provide specific, useful information for each area. Avoid duplicate pages that change only the city name.
